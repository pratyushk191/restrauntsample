      किनारा

      Kinara means shore

      Maria Fernandes grew up watching her grandmother grind recheado masala on a stone behind the house in Betim. The recipe is the same one we use now: Kashmiri chillies, garlic, cumin and toddy vinegar, with nothing from a jar.

      We opened in 2016 in a hundred-year-old house on Rua de Ourém. There are fourteen tables, a wood fire for the poee, and a kitchen that buys only what the boats bring in. If the mackerel didn't run, it isn't on the menu.

      Maria Fernandes and Anton D'Souza, owners
